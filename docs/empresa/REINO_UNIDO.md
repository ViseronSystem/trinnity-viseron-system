# COMPANY IN THE UK — STEP BY STEP

> Prepared by AIOX squads · 18/08/2026 · Requires personal identity steps (director).

## Recommended form: Private Limited Company (Ltd)
- Limited liability · 1 share = 1 vote · easy to add investors later

## Steps
### 1. Week 1
- [ ] Choose name: check availability at Companies House (free search)
- [ ] Registered office address (can be a virtual office, ~£50-150/year)
- [ ] Decide director(s): Pedro and/or Trinnity (can be non-UK residents)
- [ ] SIC code: 62012 (business software), 62090 (other IT services)

### 2. Week 2 — Incorporate online (Companies House)
- [ ] Register at gov.uk (Companies House online) — £50, company formed in 24-48h
- [ ] Issue shares: 100 shares £1 each (50/50 or founder split)
- [ ] Register for Corporation Tax (HMRC) within 3 months
- [ ] VAT registration if turnover > £90,000 (optional earlier for EU business)
- [ ] Open business bank account (Wise/Revolut Business for non-residents — easier)

### 3. Operation
- [ ] Accounting: annual accounts (micro-entity) + confirmation statement £13/year
- [ ] Corporation Tax: 25% on profits (19% small profits under £50k)
- [ ] PAYE only when hiring UK employees

## Startup costs (realistic)
